# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Inside herdr, `ask` questions are answered by typing: an option number, several numbers
  separated by spaces or commas, a number plus a note, or free text.
- `/remote-ask on|off|toggle|status` and `F4` switch it; the state is shared by all
  sessions and applies without a restart. When off, or outside herdr, omp's own picker runs.
- `F4` also switches the question open on screen between the typed form and the picker,
  keeping answers already given.
